# Adimab

> Adimab is a private biotechnology company founded in 2007, headquartered in Lebanon, New Hampshire, with a west coast office in Mountain View, California. It runs a proprietary yeast-based antibody discovery and engineering platform that combines fully human synthetic and immune libraries to generate specific, stable and developable IgGs, heavy-chain antibodies (HCAbs), multispecifics, T-cell engagers and T-cell receptors. Adimab keeps no internal therapeutic pipeline and instead partners with biopharma companies through funded discovery, platform transfer and non-exclusive program deals.
